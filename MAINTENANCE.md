## Publishing

Repository: `nexobyte-tech-hq/nexobyte-tech-hq.github.io`.

Place files directly at the root of the `main` branch. Under **Settings → Pages**, select **Deploy from a branch → main → /(root)**.

After committing a change, check deployment in **Actions**. The website is published at https://nexobyte-tech-hq.github.io/.
